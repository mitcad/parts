# Mitcad parts

Mitcad's library of parts: ISO metric screws, nuts and washers, each standard
one design with a table of its sizes; and the BUILD tab's slot profiles,
connectors, nuts, caps, panels and linear parts, parametric (a profile is
cut to any length), each naming its supplier and part number; timber
members, wood fasteners, brackets, joist hangers, post bases and sheets in
industry-standard sizes, modelled from their dimensions; round,
square and rectangular steel tubes in the sizes of EN 10219-2 and
EN 10305-5, round stainless tubes in the sizes of EN ISO 1127, square
and rectangular steel tubes to ASTM A500 (HSS) in inch sizes, and a
square and a rectangular steel tube of any size you type.

In Mitcad: Libraries, add this repository's URL, Fetch; then Insert
Component from Library, or the BUILD tab's pickers. Designs record the
version they use.

The screws, nuts, washers and wood fasteners have mount frames: with a
hole's rim or round face selected (or clicked), Insert from Library seats
one there, its head or bearing face on the face, joined to the hole.

This repository was the fastener library (mitcad-fasteners) up to 1.0.0;
from 2.0.0 it is the parts library. Designs made with the fastener library
keep reading their parts at the versions they recorded.

What the recent versions added (Update in MitCad's library browser fetches
the newest):

- 2.11.0: the square and rectangular steel tubes to ASTM A500 (HSS) in inch
  sizes (search A500 or HSS), and a square and a rectangular steel tube of
  any size you type.
- 2.10.0: the mount frames of the screws, nuts and washers.
- 2.9.0: each fastener one component of its ISO and DIN standard, its rows
  tagged with their standards and the suppliers' articles.
- 2.8.0: the ISO screws, nuts and washers M1.6 to M2.5 and M14 to M24.
- 2.7.0: the steel tubes, round, square and rectangular to EN 10219-2,
  square and rectangular to EN 10305-5, and round stainless tubes in the
  sizes of EN ISO 1127.
